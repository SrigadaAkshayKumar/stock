Indian equity prices respond to several broad factors in addition to company results.

Monetary policy: Reserve Bank of India interest-rate decisions and liquidity conditions affect valuations, especially for banks and rate-sensitive sectors.

Foreign flows: buying or selling by foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) can move large-cap stocks such as Reliance, TCS and Infosys.

Currency: a weaker rupee generally supports export-oriented IT services companies, while a stronger rupee can pressure their margins.

Commodities: crude oil prices affect India's import bill, inflation and refining and petrochemical businesses.

Global risk sentiment: US interest rates, US technology spending and global growth expectations influence Indian IT services stocks in particular.

Events: quarterly earnings seasons, the Union Budget, elections and index rebalancing can cause short-term volatility.
