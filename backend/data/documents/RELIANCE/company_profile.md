Reliance Industries is an Indian conglomerate headquartered in Mumbai and listed on the NSE and BSE.

Business segments: oil-to-chemicals (refining and petrochemicals), oil and gas exploration and production, retail (Reliance Retail) and digital services (Jio Platforms, telecom and digital businesses). It is also investing in new energy such as solar and hydrogen.

Key drivers to monitor: gross refining margins and petrochemical spreads, retail store expansion and revenue growth, telecom subscriber growth and average revenue per user (ARPU), capital expenditure and net debt, and updates on new-energy projects.

Key risks: volatility in crude oil prices and refining margins, execution risk on large capital projects, regulatory and tariff changes in telecom, competition in retail and telecom, and conglomerate complexity affecting valuation.
