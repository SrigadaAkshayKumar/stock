Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is an Indian information technology services and consulting company and part of the Tata Group. It is headquartered in Mumbai and listed on the NSE and BSE.

Business: TCS provides IT services, consulting, digital transformation, cloud, data and engineering services and business process services to large enterprises. Banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI), retail, manufacturing, life sciences and communications are important verticals. North America and Europe account for a large share of revenue.

Key drivers to monitor: quarterly revenue growth in constant currency, operating margin, deal wins and total contract value, headcount and attrition, and commentary on client technology budgets.

Key risks: slowdown in client spending in the US and Europe, pricing pressure, exchange-rate movements of the Indian rupee against the US dollar, euro and pound, wage inflation and attrition, regulatory changes on work visas, and disruption of traditional services by automation and generative AI.
