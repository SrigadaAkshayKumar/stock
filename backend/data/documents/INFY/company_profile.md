Infosys is an Indian information technology services and consulting company headquartered in Bengaluru. Its shares are listed on the NSE and BSE, and its American Depositary Receipts trade on the NYSE.

Business: Infosys offers digital services, consulting, cloud, application development and maintenance, and outsourcing services. Financial services, retail, communications, energy and manufacturing are key verticals, with North America and Europe as the largest markets.

Key drivers to monitor: revenue growth guidance for the fiscal year, operating margin guidance, large deal wins, utilisation and attrition, and management commentary on discretionary spending.

Key risks: revisions to revenue guidance, weak discretionary technology spending, client concentration in financial services, currency movements, wage inflation, visa and immigration rules, and competitive pressure from automation and AI-led delivery models.
